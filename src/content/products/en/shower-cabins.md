## What Are UPVC Shower Cabins?

UPVC shower cabins are prefabricated bathroom enclosures built from
waterproof PVC wall panels and framing, offering a hygienic, low-maintenance
alternative to fully tiled shower stalls. The panels are manufactured with
sealed, moisture-proof joints that prevent water seepage into walls — one of
the most common sources of bathroom damage over time.

## Key Benefits

- **100% water resistance** — UPVC panels never absorb moisture, so there is
  no risk of mold growth behind tiles or grout deterioration.
- **Easy to clean** — the smooth, non-porous surface wipes clean without
  harsh chemicals or scrubbing.
- **Fast installation** — prefabricated panels install significantly faster
  than tiling a shower stall from scratch, minimizing renovation downtime.
- **Modern finishes** — available in a range of colors and textures to match
  contemporary bathroom designs.
- **Long-term durability** — resistant to cracking, chipping, and discoloration
  even under daily use and cleaning.

## Materials & Construction

Our shower cabins are built from solid UPVC wall panels with tongue-and-groove
or sealed-joint assembly, paired with tempered safety glass doors or panels
where applicable, and corrosion-resistant hardware for hinges, handles, and
tracks. All joints are sealed with waterproof silicone to guarantee a fully
watertight enclosure.

## Common Use Cases

UPVC shower cabins are an excellent fit for family bathrooms, rental
properties, hotels, and any space where low maintenance and water resistance
are top priorities. They're also a practical renovation option for
replacing aging tiled showers without a full bathroom teardown.
