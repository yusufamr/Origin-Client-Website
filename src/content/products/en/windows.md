## What Are UPVC Windows?

UPVC (Unplasticized Polyvinyl Chloride) windows are manufactured from a rigid,
weather-resistant plastic profile reinforced internally with galvanized steel
for structural strength. Unlike traditional wood or aluminum frames, UPVC
does not rot, rust, warp, or require repainting — making it one of the most
practical window materials available for modern homes and commercial
buildings.

Every UPVC window we manufacture is fitted with multi-chamber profiles and
double- or triple-glazed glass units, sealed with high-quality gaskets to
eliminate drafts and moisture infiltration.

## Key Benefits

- **Thermal insulation** — multi-chamber profiles and sealed glazing reduce
  heat transfer, keeping interiors cooler in summer and warmer in winter,
  which can noticeably lower energy bills.
- **Sound reduction** — the dense profile and sealed glazing units
  significantly cut down on outside noise, ideal for homes near busy streets.
- **Weather resistance** — UPVC does not rust, rot, or corrode, and holds up
  reliably against sun, humidity, and rain year-round.
- **Low maintenance** — no painting or sealing required; a simple wipe-down
  keeps frames looking new for years.
- **Security** — multi-point locking systems and reinforced steel cores make
  UPVC windows significantly harder to force open than standard frames.

## Materials & Construction

Our UPVC windows are built from virgin PVC profiles reinforced with
galvanized steel inserts at load-bearing points, fitted with EPDM rubber
gaskets for a weathertight seal, and glazed with double-pane (or optional
triple-pane) insulated glass units. Every profile is UV-stabilized to resist
yellowing and brittleness from prolonged sun exposure.

## Common Use Cases

UPVC windows are suited to virtually any building type: residential homes,
apartments, villas, offices, and retail storefronts. They are particularly
popular in coastal or high-humidity regions where wood and metal frames
deteriorate quickly, and in urban areas where sound insulation from traffic
noise is a priority. Available in sliding, casement, tilt-and-turn, and fixed
configurations to match any architectural style.
